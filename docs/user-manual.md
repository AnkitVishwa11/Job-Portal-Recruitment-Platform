# Job Portal - User Manual

## Getting Started

### Registration

1. Navigate to the application URL
2. Click "Register" or "Sign Up"
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Confirm Password
   - Select Role (Job Seeker or Recruiter)
   - Phone Number (optional)
4. Click "Create Account"
5. You will be automatically logged in and redirected to your dashboard

### Login

1. Click "Login" or "Sign In"
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"
4. You will be redirected to your role-specific dashboard

## Job Seeker Guide

### Profile Management

Access your profile by clicking on your name/avatar in the navigation bar and selecting "Profile".

**Profile Sections:**
- **Personal Information**: Edit name, email, phone, and avatar
- **Resume Upload**: Upload your resume (PDF, DOC, or DOCX, max 5MB)
- **Change Password**: Update your password

### Searching for Jobs

1. Navigate to "Jobs" in the navigation bar
2. Use the search filters to refine your results:
   - **Search**: Keywords (title, company, skills)
   - **Location**: City or remote
   - **Employment Type**: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Internship
   - **Work Type**: Remote, On-site, Hybrid
   - **Experience Level**: Entry, Mid, Senior, Lead, Executive
   - **Salary Range**: Minimum and maximum salary
3. Sort results by: Newest, Oldest, Salary (High to Low), Salary (Low to High)
4. Click on any job to view details

### Applying for Jobs

1. Open a job listing
2. Click "Apply Now"
3. Write a cover letter (optional but recommended)
4. Upload your resume if not already uploaded
5. Click "Submit Application"
6. You will receive a confirmation notification

### Saving Jobs

1. While viewing a job listing, click the bookmark/save icon
2. Access saved jobs from your dashboard or "Saved Jobs" page
3. Remove saved jobs by clicking the bookmark icon again
4. Add personal notes to saved jobs

### Tracking Applications

1. Visit your dashboard
2. View recent applications in the "Recent Applications" section
3. See application status: Pending, Reviewing, Shortlisted, Rejected, Hired
4. Withdraw applications if needed
5. Click "View All" to see full application history

### Dashboard Features

- **Total Applications**: Number of jobs you've applied to
- **Pending Review**: Applications awaiting recruiter review
- **Shortlisted**: Applications selected for further consideration
- **Hired**: Successful applications
- **Recent Applications**: Quick view of your latest 5 applications
- **Quick Actions**: Links to search jobs, view applications, saved jobs
- **Profile Status**: Profile completeness indicator

## Recruiter Guide

### Company Profile

1. Navigate to "My Company" from your dashboard
2. **Create Company**: Fill in company details (name, description, industry, website, size, etc.)
3. **Edit Company**: Update company information at any time
4. **Delete Company**: Remove your company profile (only if no active jobs)

### Posting Jobs

1. Click "Post a Job" from dashboard
2. Fill in job details:
   - **Title**: Job position title
   - **Description**: Full job description (include requirements, responsibilities)
   - **Location**: Work location
   - **Employment Type**: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Internship
   - **Work Type**: Remote, On-site, Hybrid
   - **Experience Level**: Entry, Mid, Senior, Lead, Executive
   - **Salary Range**: Minimum and maximum salary
   - **Skills**: Required skills (comma-separated)
   - **Benefits**: Benefits offered
3. Click "Post Job"

### Managing Jobs

1. Access "My Jobs" from dashboard
2. View all posted jobs with status indicator
3. **Edit**: Update job details
4. **Close**: Stop accepting applications (job remains visible but closed)
5. **Delete**: Remove job permanently

### Reviewing Applications

1. Click on any job to view its applications
2. See list of applicants with names, applied dates, and status
3. Click "Review" to see full application details
4. **Download Resume**: Download applicant's resume

### Application Management

For each application, you can:

1. **Shortlist**: Mark applicant as shortlisted for interview
2. **Reject**: Reject with optional reason/notes
3. **Hire**: Mark applicant as hired
4. **Status Update**: Manually update application status

### Dashboard Features

- **Total Jobs**: Number of jobs you've posted
- **Active Jobs**: Currently open positions
- **Total Applications**: All received applications
- **New Applicants**: Applications not yet reviewed
- **Recent Applications**: Latest 5 applications with quick actions
- **Active Jobs List**: Quick overview of your open positions
- **Quick Actions**: Links to post job, manage jobs, view applicants

## Password Management

### Change Password

1. Go to your Profile
2. Click "Change Password"
3. Enter current password
4. Enter new password (minimum 8 characters)
5. Confirm new password
6. Click "Update Password"
7. You will need to log in again

### Forgot Password

1. On the login page, click "Forgot Password?"
2. Enter your registered email
3. Check your email for a reset link/token
4. Enter the token and your new password
5. Log in with your new password

## Notifications

Notifications are displayed in the notification bell icon in the navigation bar.

**Types of Notifications:**
- Application status changes
- New job matches
- Application received (recruiters)
- Profile updates

**Actions:**
- Click on a notification to view details
- Mark individual notifications as read
- Mark all notifications as read
- Delete notifications
